What features does freehabittracker.app offer?

How does habit tracking help build better habits?

Behavioral science shows that tracking habits creates a powerful visual feedback loop. When you mark a habit complete and watch your streak number climb, your brain gets a small dopamine reward that reinforces the behavior. This is the foundation of the "don't break the chain" method popularized by Jerry Seinfeld — the visual record of consecutive days creates social proof with yourself. Research from the British Journal of Health Psychology found that people who tracked their exercise were significantly more likely to maintain the habit than those who didn't. The key insight is that consistency matters far more than perfection. Missing one day doesn't erase your progress — the calendar and heatmap show that even imperfect weeks contain completed days. By making your effort visible, a habit tracker shifts the question from "should I do this today?" to "do I want to keep my streak alive?" That reframe makes the daily decision almost automatic.

How do I start tracking habits for free?

Getting started takes less than thirty seconds. First, click the "+ New Habit" button in the sidebar and type a name — something like "Exercise," "Read 30 minutes," or "Meditate." Pick a color and choose how often you want to do it: daily, weekdays, weekends, or custom days. Second, come back each day and check off what you completed. The Today view shows your habits as large, tappable cards with a single-tap checkbox. You can also click any day on the calendar to mark habits retroactively. Third, watch your streaks, heatmap, and stats grow over time. The longer you track, the more motivating the data becomes. Your streak counter and completion percentage create natural accountability. If you miss a day, don't worry — just pick up where you left off. The heatmap will still show your overall consistency.

Is freehabittracker.app private and safe?

Your habit data never leaves your device. freehabittracker.app stores everything in your browser's localStorage — a private storage area that only this website can access. There is no database, no server endpoint, and no API that receives your data. No cookies are set, no fingerprinting occurs, and no analytics or tracking scripts are loaded. The app does not know who you are, what habits you track, or how often you visit. If you clear your browser data, your habits are erased completely — because there is no backup on any server. For users who want a safety net, the Export button downloads a full JSON backup to your device that you control. The app is also a Progressive Web App, meaning it can be installed on your home screen and works fully offline with no network connection required. Your habits are your business, and freehabittracker.app keeps it that way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is freehabittracker.app really free?

Yes, completely free with no limits. Track unlimited habits, view full stats, export your data, and use every feature at no cost. An optional premium plan adds cloud sync and charts for $2.88/month, but the core tracker will always be free.

No account needed. Just open the site and start tracking. Your data is saved automatically in your browser's local storage. There is no sign-up form, no email verification, and no password to remember.

No. All data stays on your device in localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. There are no cookies, no analytics, and no tracking. If you want a backup, use the Export button to download your data as a JSON file.

Your streak resets but all your history is preserved. The calendar and heatmap still show every day you completed, so you can see your overall consistency. Habit science shows that bouncing back quickly matters more than never missing — just check off tomorrow and keep going.

Yes. The app is fully responsive with a dedicated mobile layout, bottom navigation bar, and touch-friendly habit cards. It also works offline as a Progressive Web App — add it to your home screen for an app-like experience without downloading anything from an app store.
